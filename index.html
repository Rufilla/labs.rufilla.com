A Raspberry Pi-sized carrier board breaking the LuckFox Core3506 module out to Ethernet, two USB-C ports, DSI and a 40-pin header. Revision A is built, reworked, and honest about its errata.

A Pi Zero form-factor HAT that hands any 40-pin host Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5 LE, Zigbee and Thread from an ESP32-C6. Hardware design only — bring your own firmware.
